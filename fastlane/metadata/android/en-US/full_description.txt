Nomla is a client for the Mumble voice chat system. Host your own server or join one of the many community-run public servers (see https://www.mumble.info).

Nomla was ported from Mumla and almost completely rewritten. Mumla and its protocol library Humla are by Daniel Lublin and contributors; they continue Plumble and Jumble by Andrew Comminos and contributors.

Some of the features:

* Certificate generation, import and export
* Browse public servers
* Voice activated transmission (the default), or different Push-to-talk buttons
* Adaptive voice detection threshold, with a live input level meter in the settings
* Noise suppression with RNNoise, switchable during a call
* Echo cancellation (WebRTC AEC3) that follows the audio device and can be switched per type of device
* Audio device picker like the phone app: Bluetooth, speaker, earpiece (screen turns off at your ear), wired or USB headset
* Bluetooth headsets are used automatically
* Self-registration on server
* Access tokens
* Opus codec support
* Automatic reconnection, which keeps the microphone working with the screen off
* Chat images shown scaled down, tap for fullscreen
* Tor support through the Orbot app
* Text-to-speech for messages (turned on by default)
* Light and dark theme
